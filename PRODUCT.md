# StudyMitra
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
React with Vite; frontend-only migration requested by the user. Existing FastAPI API contracts remain unchanged.
## Users
Students studying from their own PDFs, notes, textbooks and previous-year questions.
## Product Purpose
Upload academic resources and ask questions with grounded, cited answers.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve explain, exam answer, summarize, compare, PYQs, quiz, filters, document metadata, reindexing, deletion, sources, streaming and history. Backend belongs to the user's friend and is out of scope. User confirmed local browser document storage with clearly offline AI features when disconnected. Never fabricate an AI answer.
## Brand Commitments
Blue family with 5–6 gradient colors, readable light and dark themes, Phosphor icons, playful student language and a focus switch that quiets personality without removing features. Organized source folders. No Sites skill.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readability above color, keyboard-accessible controls, reduced motion and responsive layouts.
